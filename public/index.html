How automatic rules work

Missed bid: treated as 0.

All zero: random player with a free slot gets the name.

Budget reaches 0: paid bidding ends for that player.

Final leftovers: when the remaining names exactly match bankrupt players’ empty slots, those names are filled automatically so everyone finishes with 6.
